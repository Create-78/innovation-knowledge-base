## Reading across the rows

- Centers that lasted decades had a **funding base tied to the parent's revenue or a government mission**, not discretionary budget (Bell Labs, DARPA, Skunk Works, GE/DuPont).
- The recurring failure is at **hand-off**, not at invention: PARC, DARPA transition, Corfam/IBM relational database.
- Weaker form of the same pattern: **ownership or budget shocks** coincide with decline (Bell Labs, Google X's cuts, MSR closure).
- Most causal claims are single-case. The best quasi-experimental evidence in the base concerns **funding structure** (HHMI vs. NIH) and **IP licensing** (1956 AT&T decree).
